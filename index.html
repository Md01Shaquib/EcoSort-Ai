Configure Your AI Model

Train a waste classification model on Google Teachable Machine, export it for TensorFlow.js, and paste the model URL below.

How to train and export your model
  1. Go to Teachable Machine Image Project
  2. Create classes: Plastic Bottle, Cardboard, Paper, Metal Can, Glass Bottle, Fruit Peels, Vegetable Scraps, Food Waste, Leaves, Mobile Phone, Charger, Circuit Board, Battery, Plastic Bag
  3. Add 50+ training images per class using your webcam or by uploading photos
  4. Click "Train Model" and wait for training to complete
  5. Test the model in the preview panel to verify accuracy
  6. Click "Export Model" → select "TensorFlow.js" → click "Upload my model"
  7. Copy the shareable link (it starts with https://teachablemachine.withgoogle.com/models/...)
  8. Paste the URL in the field above and click "Load Model"

Tip: Use diverse images — different angles, lighting, and backgrounds improve accuracy. Aim for 100+ images per class for best results.
